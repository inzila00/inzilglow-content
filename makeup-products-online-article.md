## ⭐ 1. Understand Your Skin Type
Knowing your skin type is the first and most important step before buying any makeup product. Every formula reacts differently depending on your skin.

- **Dry Skin:** Look for hydrating creams, dewy foundations, moisturizing primers, and butter-like lip formulas.  
- **Oily Skin:** Choose matte foundations, oil-control powders, gel-based primers, and long-wear products.  
- **Sensitive Skin:** Avoid fragrance-heavy or alcohol-based products. Always check for “hypoallergenic” or “dermatologically tested” labels.  
- **Combination Skin:** Choose multi-use products that balance hydration and oil control.

---

## ⭐ 2. Check Ingredients Before Buying
Ingredients play a major role in how a product affects your skin.

- For dry skin, products with **hyaluronic acid, glycerin, ceramides**, and **vitamin E** work best.  
- For oily or acne-prone skin, choose formulas with **niacinamide, salicylic acid**, or lightweight, non-comedogenic ingredients.  
- For sensitive skin, avoid parabens, sulphates, artificial fragrances, and harsh chemicals.

Reading ingredient lists helps you avoid unwanted reactions and pick what’s truly safe for your skin.
